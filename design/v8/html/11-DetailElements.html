Detail elements

The pulled-up sheet is built from these cards, in a fixed order. A layer picks the cards it needs and fills them; the shell owns the subject, the In view line and the footer.

Standard order

1SubjectShellWhere the pin is and its elevation. Always present.
2HeadlineLayerOne value and one sentence: what it means at the pin now.
3Key statsLayer2 to 4 values with units and a short note.
4Table or visualLayerBy elevation, by time, or a small chart. One idea per card.
5Second visualLayerOnly if the layer needs one.
6Status linesLayerEvents at the pin: freeze-thaw, inversion, a warning.
7ExpandablesLayerDetail one tap away, closed by default.
8Coming laterLayerDashed placeholder for planned data.
9In viewShellOne line about the whole map view.
10FooterShellModel, run age, elevation used, method, Layer settings. Later, the Full forecast button.

Every card: 12 radius, white at 4.5%, 12 padding, a small caps title, optional meta on the right. Cards a layer doesn’t need are left out, but the order never changes. The sheet scrolls: the scrubber scrolls away and the subject stays pinned. Cards keep to the phone width, 358 px.
