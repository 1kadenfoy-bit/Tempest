# Weather on the map: the Freezing line and Rain/snow line buttons

2026-11-26 · branch `claude/tempest-weather` · ready to integrate

## What changed
The two buttons the Interface chat added to the map panel ("Freezing line" and
"Rain/snow line") now draw their lines. **Please keep all of this when
integrating.**

- **Freezing line:** dashed, at 0 °C, labelled with its height.
- **Rain/snow line:** solid, labelled "Snow line" with its height (where the wet-bulb temperature falls to 0.5 °C).
- Both can be on at once. They show on the **temperature** and **precipitation** maps. Both are off until the button is tapped (the panel remembers the choice).
- A tap redraws at once; the lines follow the light and dark theme.
- **The old per-layer line settings are removed** so each line has one control:
  Temperature's "Freezing level line" toggle and Precipitation's "Line on the
  map" (Off / Snow line / 0 °C). Do not bring them back from older code.
- **Precipitation colours now always follow the wet-bulb rule** (before, "Off"
  and "0 °C" fell back to the model's own rain/snow split).
- Turning on the rain/snow line on the temperature map also asks for humidity
  at the map nodes (the same request the precipitation map already makes).

Picture: `2026-11-26-map-lines-temperature.png` (390 × 844, recorded weather, both lines on, zoomed to Howe Sound area). There is no v8 screen for these lines.

## What to try after it is integrated
1. Open Map panel, tap "Freezing line": a dashed line appears on Temperature and Precipitation. Tap again: it goes.
2. Tap "Rain/snow line": a solid line with "Snow line · … m". Both on: both visible.
3. Reload: the buttons keep their state.
4. Open Temperature and Precipitation settings: no line options there any more.
5. Switch to Wind or Radar: no lines.
