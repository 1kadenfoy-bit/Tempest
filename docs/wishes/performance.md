# Kaden's rules: Performance

How Kaden wants this section to behave and look, in his words where possible.
The Performance chat keeps this list up to date (`docs/sections/README.md`). One rule per
line, numbered, with the date. When he changes his mind, the rule is edited and
the old wording moves to "Changed".

## Targets

1. The app loads in under 7 seconds, with everything loaded. (Nov 27, 2026)
2. Pan, pinch and tilt: at least 30 frames a second, and the slowest 1% no lower than 24. (Nov 27, 2026)
3. Dragging the time slider or playing keeps up with the map. (Nov 27, 2026)
4. Tapping buttons feels instant: 0.1 to 0.2 seconds. (Nov 27, 2026)
5. Switching layers takes under 1 second, at most a momentary hitch. (Nov 27, 2026)
6. The app only works while it is being used. (Nov 27, 2026)
7. Memory stays under what works for Safari. (Nov 27, 2026)
8. Things must feel snappy: clicking and panning feel "live". (Nov 27, 2026)
